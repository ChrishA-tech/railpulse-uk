# TransPennine Express Passenger Charter — Delay Repay 15

## [TOC-TPE-DR15] TransPennine Express Delay Repay 15-Minute Rule
TransPennine Express (TPE) operates Delay Repay 15 across trans-Pennine and Anglo-Scottish corridors (`LIV`, `MAN`, `LDS`, `YRK`, `NCL`, `EDB`, `GLC`, `SHF`):
- **15 to 29 minutes delay**: 25% of single fare (12.5% of return fare).
- **30 to 59 minutes delay**: 50% of single fare (25% of return fare).
- **60 to 119 minutes delay**: 100% of single fare (50% of return fare).
- **120+ minutes delay**: 100% of single or return fare.
