# Network Rail Delay Attribution Guide — Statutory Code Reference

## [NR-DAG-CODES] Primary & Reactionary Delay Attribution Codes
- **ON_TIME**: Service running on schedule or within sub-threshold tolerance.
- **TG**: Traincrew / driver diagramming or availability delay.
- **X8**: External severe weather (named storm, high wind speed restrictions, low railhead adhesion, flooding, freezing points).
- **I1**: Track, points, or rail infrastructure defect.
- **I2**: Signalling system, track circuit, or axle counter failure.
- **M2**: Rolling stock mechanical or traction fleet fault.
- **YI**: Reactionary / knock-on delay propagated from an earlier delayed service.
- **OT**: Overhead line equipment (OLE) power supply disruption.
- **XD**: External emergency, trespass, or police incident.
