# National Rail Conditions of Travel (NRCoT) — Statutory Excerpts

## [NRCoT-14.2] Condition 14.2 — Split Ticketing Validity
A passenger may use two or more tickets for one continuous journey provided the train is scheduled to call at every station where one ticket ends and the next ticket begins, unless an exception under Condition 14.3 applies. If the service does not call at the split station, the split-ticket combination is invalid for that train.

## [NRCoT-14.3] Condition 14.3 — Zonal & Season Ticket Boundary Exception
Where one of the tickets held is a Season Ticket, Travelcard, or zonal ticket covering the relevant boundary zone, the train does not need to call at the boundary station provided the combination of tickets covers the entire journey route.

## [NRCoT-28.1] Condition 28.1 — Delay Repay & Through-Journey Split-Ticket Protection
Under Condition 28 and ORR through-journey guidance, compensation is calculated against the total delay to the passenger's end-to-end journey across a valid combination of split tickets, provided minimum interchange connection times are respected. Claims must be submitted to the Train Operating Company (TOC) responsible for the delay within 28 days of completing the journey.

## [ORR-DISRUPTION-32] Condition 32 — Severe Disruption, Ticket Acceptance & Force Majeure
Under NRCoT Condition 32 and Office of Rail and Road (ORR) enforcement, Train Operating Companies must provide alternative transport or mutual ticket acceptance during severe disruption and may not withhold Delay Repay compensation for extraordinary weather events (Network Rail Delay Attribution code X8) or infrastructure failures.
