# London North Eastern Railway (LNER) Passenger Charter — Delay Repay 30

## [TOC-LNER-DR30] LNER Delay Repay 30-Minute Rule
LNER operates a 30-minute Delay Repay threshold on East Coast Main Line services (`KGX`, `PBO`, `YRK`, `LDS`, `NCL`, `EDB`):
- **Under 30 minutes delay (including 15–29 minutes)**: 0% compensation under LNER's 30-minute charter.
- **30 to 59 minutes delay**: 50% of single fare (25% of return fare).
- **60 to 119 minutes delay**: 100% of single fare (50% of return fare).
- **120+ minutes delay**: 100% of single or return fare.
