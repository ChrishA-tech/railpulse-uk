# Avanti West Coast Passenger Charter — Delay Repay 15

## [TOC-AVANTI-DR15] Avanti West Coast Delay Repay 15-Minute Rule
Avanti West Coast operates Delay Repay 15 across West Coast Main Line services (`EUS`, `BHM`, `CRE`, `MAN`, `LIV`, `PRE`, `GLC`):
- **15 to 29 minutes delay**: 25% of a single ticket fare (12.5% of a return ticket fare).
- **30 to 59 minutes delay**: 50% of a single ticket fare (25% of a return ticket fare).
- **60 to 119 minutes delay**: 100% of a single ticket fare (50% of a return ticket fare).
- **120+ minutes delay**: 100% of a single or return ticket fare.
Claims must be lodged within 28 days of the delayed journey.
